Sharpe compares average return above T-bills with the variability of those monthly returns; Calmar compares annualised real growth with the largest observed loss from a peak. A strong Sharpe can coexist with a very deep drawdown—it does not tell you how painful the worst decline felt.

How the verdicts are assigned

Negative real annual return: “Loses to inflation”. Otherwise, drawdown worse than −40%: “Positive return, deep drawdowns”; −15% to −40%: “Grows, with drawdown risk”; better than −15%: “Preserved purchasing power”. These describe this historical sample, not future safety.

Short-cycle signal

    Long debt cycle

    Debt denomination, maturity, private versus public balance sheets, external funding and policy capacity need a deeper country study. Household debt service is not total-economy debt service.

    How this model reads the debt cycle

    Credit can raise spending faster than income until repayment and funding constraints bind. A short-cycle slowdown is different from long-term deleveraging. Dalio’s framework considers debt restructuring, spending restraint, transfers and monetary financing; this panel does not mechanically date those phases.

    Short-cycle stress flags use a high-yield spread ≥5 percentage points or a three-month unemployment average ≥0.5 points above its trailing 12-month minimum. These are our educational thresholds, not Dalio’s published stage rules or an official recession determination. Rate moves of at least ±0.25 points over three months distinguish easing and tightening.

    Daily observations older than 10 days, monthly periods more than 75 days past period end and quarterly periods more than 200 days past period end are excluded from current classification. Sources have different release dates and revisions; this is not a simultaneous live snapshot or a backtested predictive model.

    05

    Follow the mechanism, question the claim

    News & Impact Chains

    Publisher headlines → possible transmission channels → gold and Bitcoin → evidence that could change the conclusion.

    Hourly scheduled scans; this page checks for published updates every five minutes while visible. This is not continuous real-time monitoring. Open the original source before treating a headline as fact. Mechanism chains are rule-based hypotheses; they are not full-article summaries, price predictions or verified causal effects.

    News source health & coverage

      Only dated, relevant headlines in the last 14 days are retained, capped at 60. Matching uses headline keywords and can miss context, negation or important stories. A fetched feed can have zero relevant items. Articles are not ranked by expected investment return.
